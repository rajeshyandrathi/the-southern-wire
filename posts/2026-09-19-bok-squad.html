Erasmus splits Bok squad ahead of rivalry series opener

Sport Desk·Saturday, 19 September 2026

Springbok coach Rassie Erasmus has named a 43-player squad for the newly minted Rugby Greatest Rivalry series against New Zealand, with the campaign set to open at Ellis Park following a warm-up test in Buenos Aires against Argentina. The size of the squad reflects the scale of the assignment: a multi-match series against the All Blacks layered on top of an already congested end-of-year international window.

The team sheet brings welcome news on the injury front. Locks Eben Etzebeth and Lood de Jager both return from spells on the sidelines, restoring depth to a pack that has carried a heavy workload this season. Flyhalf Sacha Feinberg-Mngomezulu is also back in the fold, giving Erasmus options at a position that has seen plenty of rotation, while captain Siya Kolisi returns to lead the side.

True to his reputation for tactical unpredictability, Erasmus has also released several fringe players from the squad early so they can return to franchise duty, a move that keeps his depth options sharp for Super Rugby and URC commitments while trusting his first-choice combinations to carry the series opener. It is a familiar pattern for a coach who has repeatedly prioritised long-term squad management over short-term selection headlines.

New Zealand will arrive with their own selection questions to answer, and the opening test at Ellis Park — a ground with a formidable reputation for Springbok performances — sets the tone for what promises to be a hard-fought series between the two most successful nations in the sport's history.
