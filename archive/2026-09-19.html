Fear grips Ekurhuleni as ninth body found in serial killings probe

Nine women have been found dead across Ekurhuleni, east of Johannesburg, since the first body was discovered in Kempton Park on 17 July. The most recent victim was found in Dawn Park on 17 September. Police have named four of the women — Dineo Motapane (38), Gracious Nkomo (28), Elizabeth Moselakgomo (38) and Itumeleng Kekana (32) — and are holding at least one suspect, who appeared in court this week, while investigators work to establish whether one person or several are responsible. President Cyril Ramaphosa has ordered police to leave "no stone unturned," and a R400,000 reward has been offered for information leading to further arrests. One senior police official described the hunt in stark terms: "This is a war — individuals are being hunted."

Pretoria pushes revised offer to cut 30% US tariff

South Africa's cabinet has approved an enhanced trade proposal aimed at reducing the 30% US tariff applied to South African exports since August 2025, with Trade Minister Parks Tau saying the documents would go to Washington immediately. The tariffs put an estimated 30,000 jobs at risk in the automotive and agricultural sectors, while Pretoria courts alternative markets in China and Japan.
