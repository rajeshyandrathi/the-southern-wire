Hunger Games set to rule Thanksgiving as Hexed and Focker In-Law head to SA cinemas on Friday

Disney’s original animation Hexed and the Meet the Parents sequel Focker In-Law open in the US today, each tipped to take $40 million to $45 million over the five-day holiday. Both reach South African cinemas on 27 November.

Culture Desk·Wednesday, 25 November 2026·3 min read

Key points

  • The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is projected at $80 million to $90 million over the US five-day holiday
  • Hexed and Focker In-Law are each projected at $40 million to $45 million
  • Both films open in South African cinemas on Friday, 27 November
  • Avengers: Doomsday and Dune: Part Three are listed for 16 December in South Africa

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is expected to stay on top of the North American box office over the US Thanksgiving holiday, which starts today. After its $120 million opening last weekend, the film is projected to take $80 million to $90 million over the five days from Wednesday to Sunday, Yahoo Entertainment reported, citing industry tracking.

The real contest is for second place. Disney’s Hexed and Universal’s Focker In-Law both open in the US on Wednesday, 25 November, and each is projected at $40 million to $45 million over five days. South Africans will not have long to wait: both films open here on Friday, 27 November, according to Ster-Kinekor and Nu Metro’s listings.

Other new US releases are much smaller. Angel Studios’ Hershey is tipped at $6 million to $8 million over four days, and Netflix’s The Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, with Brad Pitt following up Once Upon a Time in Hollywood, is expected to take single-digit millions from 250 IMAX screens.

A big bet for Disney

Hexed is an original story about a teenage girl who gains magical powers and travels from the suburbs to Hexe, the world of witches. It is directed by Fawn Veerasunthorn, who made Wish, and Jason Hand, with Hailee Steinfeld, Rashida Jones, Jodie Foster, Walton Goggins and Stephen Fry in the voice cast, The Disney Scoop reported. It is rated PG.

The film cost more than $200 million to make, before marketing, and Disney’s recent original animations have struggled: Wish took $31.7 million over its first five days in 2023 and Strange World $18.9 million in 2022. Deadline’s higher range of $40 million to $50 million would give Disney its best opening for an original animated film since Moana, according to The Disney Scoop.

Focker In-Law brings back Ben Stiller, Robert De Niro, Teri Polo and Owen Wilson, with Ariana Grande joining the family. Greg Focker is now a parent himself and starts to fear he is turning into his father-in-law Jack, Ster-Kinekor’s preview says. The film cost $90 million, and the earlier instalments made between $310 million and $522 million worldwide. Nu Metro lists it at 106 minutes with a 13 LV age restriction.

For Grande, it is a third Thanksgiving release in a row, after Wicked in 2024 and Wicked: For Good in 2025. Disney, meanwhile, has a strong record over the holiday with sequels: Moana 2 and Zootopia 2 rank among its biggest Thanksgiving releases, which is why the performance of an original film like Hexed is being watched closely.

What else is coming to SA cinemas

Nu Metro’s listings also show Paper Tiger and Yeh Prem Mol Liya opening on 27 November. On Friday, 4 December they list Violent Night 2, The Cat in the Hat, Highway Sheila 2 and André Rieu’s Christmas concert, with Avengers: Doomsday and Dune: Part Three both arriving on Wednesday, 16 December.

In the US, this year’s Thanksgiving slate is not expected to match 2024, when the holiday frame brought in $422 million led by Moana 2, Wicked and Gladiator II. Even so, American ticket sales are running about 20% ahead of last year and are on track to pass $10 billion for the first time since cinemas closed in 2020, Yahoo Entertainment reported.
