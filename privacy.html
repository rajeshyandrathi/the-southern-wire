Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information The Southern Wire collects when you visit, and how it's used. We've tried to write it in plain language rather than boilerplate.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which stories get read and how people navigate the site, so we can cover topics readers actually want. Analytics cookies collect anonymised usage data (pages visited, approximate location at a city/region level, device type) — they do not collect your name, email, or any way to identify you personally.

You'll see a cookie banner on your first visit. Declining it means analytics and advertising scripts won't load for you; the site works identically either way.

Advertising

Once enabled, this site shows ads served through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising through Google's Ad Settings, or decline the cookie banner on this site to prevent ad personalisation here.

Newsletter

If you sign up for our daily email, we store only your email address, used solely to send that email. We don't sell or share it. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email we send, or by contacting us.

We keep your email address for as long as you remain subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we delete it within 30 days. If a subscription email bounces or goes unopened for an extended period, we may remove the address as part of routine list maintenance.

Where your data is processed

Some of the services this site relies on — Google Analytics, Google AdSense (once enabled), and Netlify, which handles our newsletter and contact form submissions — process data on servers located outside South Africa. Under POPIA, this kind of cross-border transfer is only permitted where the recipient is subject to a comparable level of data protection or other safeguards apply; the services we use are major global providers that publish their own data-protection commitments, which you can review via the links in the sections above. By using the forms on this site, you consent to this transfer.

What we don't do

  • We don't require you to create an account to read the site.
  • We don't sell personal data to third parties.
  • We don't track you across other sites ourselves (our advertising partners may, per their own policies, linked above).

Your rights

If you're in South Africa, you have rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) regarding any personal data we hold about you (in practice, just your email if you've subscribed) — including the right to know what we hold, to ask us to correct or delete it, and to object to or restrict its processing. If you're in the EU/UK, equivalent rights apply under GDPR. To exercise these, contact us via the contact page.

If you're not satisfied with how we've handled a request or a concern about your personal information, you have the right to complain to South Africa's Information Regulator: inforegulator.org.za, enquiries@inforegulator.org.za.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Published from Johannesburg, South Africa.
