Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what information The Southern Wire collects when you visit, and how it's used. We've tried to write it in plain language rather than boilerplate.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which stories get read and how people navigate the site, so we can cover topics readers actually want. Analytics cookies collect anonymised usage data (pages visited, approximate location at a city/region level, device type) — they do not collect your name, email, or any way to identify you personally.

You'll see a cookie banner on your first visit. Declining it means analytics and advertising scripts won't load for you; the site works identically either way.

Advertising

Once enabled, this site shows ads served through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising through Google's Ad Settings, or decline the cookie banner on this site to prevent ad personalisation here.

Newsletter

If you sign up for our daily email, we store only your email address, used solely to send that email. We don't sell or share it. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email we send, or by contacting us.

What we don't do

  • We don't require you to create an account to read the site.
  • We don't sell personal data to third parties.
  • We don't track you across other sites ourselves (our advertising partners may, per their own policies, linked above).

Your rights

If you're in South Africa, you have rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) regarding any personal data we hold about you (in practice, just your email if you've subscribed). If you're in the EU/UK, equivalent rights apply under GDPR. To exercise these — access, correction, or deletion — contact us via the contact page.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Published from Johannesburg, South Africa.
