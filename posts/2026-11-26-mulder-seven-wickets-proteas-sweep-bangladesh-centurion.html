Mulder takes seven for 31 as the Proteas crush Bangladesh by an innings to sweep the series

A day after his century, Wiaan Mulder ripped through Bangladesh at Centurion. South Africa won the second Test by an innings and 219 runs inside three days and took the series 2-0.

Sport Desk·Thursday, 26 November 2026·3 min read

Key points

  • South Africa beat Bangladesh by an innings and 219 runs at Centurion on day three, Wednesday 25 November
  • Bangladesh were bowled out for 184 after South Africa declared overnight on 494 for 7
  • Wiaan Mulder took 7 for 31, the best figures by a South African against Bangladesh, and was Player of the Match
  • Marques Ackerman was Player of the Series after scores of 157 and 150
  • Three ODIs start in East London on Tuesday, 1 December; England arrive for three Tests from 17 December

The second Test at SuperSport Park did not need a fourth day. South Africa declared overnight on 494 for 7, a lead of 403, and then bowled Bangladesh out for 184 midway through the afternoon session on Wednesday, 25 November. The innings and 219-run win gives the Proteas a 2-0 series sweep, SuperSport reported.

The star was Wiaan Mulder. A day after scoring 100, the all-rounder took a career-best 7 for 31, his first five-wicket haul in Tests. Fullview said it is the best return by a South African against Bangladesh, beating Keshav Maharaj’s 7 for 32. Mulder took eight wickets in the match and was named Player of the Match.

A correction to our report on Wednesday: we said Marco Jansen would resume on 67 not out and chase a first Test century on day three. In fact, South Africa declared before play, so Jansen finished unbeaten on 67.

A good start, then a collapse

Bangladesh began well. Openers Mahfijul Islam (38) and Tanzid Hasan (29) put on 58, which Fullview said is Bangladesh’s highest opening stand against South Africa. Jansen broke it by trapping Mahfijul lbw, and the visitors were 109 for 3 at lunch, according to SuperSport.

Towhid Hridoy hit a lively 41 off 33 balls before Kagiso Rabada had him caught in the gully. Captain Najmul Hossain Shanto ground out 34 from 101 balls. At 158 for 4 the game still had some life, but Mulder then ran through the middle and lower order, and Bangladesh slid to 182 for 9. Ryan Rickelton took three catches behind the stumps off Mulder’s bowling.

Jansen finished it, removing the last man, Khaled Ahmed, who was caught by Mulder on the third-man boundary.

Rare company for Mulder

SuperSport said Mulder is only the fifth South African to score a century and take five wickets in the same Test, after Jimmy Sinclair, Aubrey Faulkner, Jacques Kallis (twice) and Corbin Bosch. He is also the fourth player in Test history to make a hundred and take seven wickets in an innings in the same match, joining Ian Botham, Ravichandran Ashwin and Naseer Maroofkhil.

Mulder played down the milestones. “It’s pretty cool to contribute,” he said, according to Fullview, adding that being part of the team meant more to him than becoming a superstar.

Marques Ackerman was named Player of the Series after scores of 157 at the Wanderers and 150 at Centurion, 307 runs at a strike rate of about 100. “I put a lot of effort and work in the nets,” he said.

What comes next

South Africa have now beaten Australia 2-1 and Bangladesh 2-0 in just over a month. The white-ball leg of the tour starts with three ODIs, the first in East London (KuGompo City) on Tuesday, 1 December, followed by three T20 internationals. England then arrive for three Tests over the festive season, starting at the Wanderers on 17 December.
