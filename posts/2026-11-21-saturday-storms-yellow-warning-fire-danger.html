Weather & Snow

Severe storms, hail and lightning for much of the interior today, while the Karoo faces extreme fire danger

The weather service has issued a yellow level 2 warning for severe thunderstorms across parts of six provinces on Saturday. In the dry west, fire danger is extremely high, and KwaZulu-Natal’s coast will be hot and humid.

Weather Desk·Saturday, 21 November 2026·3 min read

Key points

  • Yellow level 2 warning for severe thunderstorms with hail, damaging winds, heavy rain and lots of lightning
  • Affected: most of the Free State, central North West, southern Gauteng, most of KZN and Mpumalanga, parts of Limpopo and the eastern Eastern Cape
  • Extremely high fire danger in the central and eastern Northern Cape, northeastern Western Cape and parts of the Eastern Cape
  • Very high UV levels; hot and uncomfortable in eastern KZN

Saturday is shaping up as a hot and stormy day across much of South Africa. The South African Weather Service (SAWS) has issued a yellow level 2 impact-based warning for severe thunderstorms that could bring heavy downpours, strong damaging winds, hail and excessive lightning, The Citizen reports.

The warning covers the Free State except the southwest, central North West, southern Gauteng, KwaZulu-Natal except its northeastern parts, most of Mpumalanga, central and southeastern Limpopo, and the eastern half of the Eastern Cape. SAWS warns that the storms could cause localised flooding of roads, low-lying areas and bridges.

The storms come after a week in which heavy rain and flash floods have already caused heartbreak. On Friday the body of an 11-year-old girl who was swept away by floodwater in Soweto was recovered from the Snake Park River in Dobsonville. Emergency services urge people never to cross flooded roads or rivers, on foot or by car.

Province by province

In Gauteng it will be partly cloudy and warm with isolated afternoon showers and thundershowers, more widespread in the south, and hot in the far north. Mpumalanga will be hot in the Lowveld, with scattered afternoon storms elsewhere. Limpopo will be hot to very hot, with isolated to scattered thundershowers.

North West and the Free State will be warm to hot with isolated to scattered afternoon storms. KwaZulu-Natal will start with fog in the interior, then turn warm to hot with scattered afternoon showers and thundershowers. SAWS has issued an advisory for hot, humid and very uncomfortable conditions along the province’s eastern parts.

The Western Cape will be mostly fine, cool to warm, and hot in the northeast. The Northern Cape will be warm to hot and windy in the south, with isolated showers in the east. The western Eastern Cape will be warm to hot with isolated thundershowers in the north, and the east of the province will see morning fog south of the escarpment, then scattered afternoon storms.

Fire danger in the dry west

While the east gets rain, the west is tinder dry. SAWS warns of extremely high fire danger over the central and eastern Northern Cape, the northeastern Western Cape, and parts of the central and northern Eastern Cape. In these conditions, any fire can spread rapidly and be very hard to control, so avoid open fires, braais in the veld and burning rubbish.

The UV index is expected to be very high, so if you are outdoors, wear a hat and sunscreen and seek shade around midday.

The outlook for Sunday is similar: partly cloudy, cool to warm but hot to very hot in places, with isolated to scattered showers and thundershowers. Check the SAWS app or website for updated warnings before travelling.
