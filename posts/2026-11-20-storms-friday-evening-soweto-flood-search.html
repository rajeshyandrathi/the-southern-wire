More storms tonight, as Soweto mourns a girl swept away by floodwater

SAWS has issued a yellow level 2 warning for severe thunderstorms on Friday evening across parts of Gauteng, North West, Mpumalanga, KwaZulu-Natal and the northern Northern Cape. In Soweto, a four-day search for an 11-year-old ended in tragedy.

Weather Desk·Friday, 20 November 2026

The South African Weather Service has issued a yellow level 2 warning for severe thunderstorms from Friday evening into the night. The warning covers parts of Gauteng, North West, Mpumalanga and KwaZulu-Natal, as well as the northern Northern Cape including Upington.

SAWS says the storms could bring heavy downpours that lead to localised flooding, along with damaging winds, hail and a lot of lightning. Forecasters had already warned earlier in the week that Thursday’s quieter weather would not last, and that thunderstorms could turn severe again over the weekend.

The warning lands in a week when Soweto has seen what flash floods can do. Earlier this week, 11-year-old Anqobile Jiyela was swept away by floodwater in Dobsonville shortly after she was dropped off by her scholar transport. City emergency teams searched for four days, clearing thick vegetation by hand to reach one area. On Friday, the search ended in tragedy after a body was recovered, and the family is awaiting formal confirmation.

The City of Johannesburg says heavy rain and flash flooding damaged several Soweto areas, including Zola, Dobsonville, Moroka, Chiawelo, Klipspruit, Pimville and Nancefield, and that a multidisciplinary response is still running in the region.

If you are in a warning area tonight, the basic advice stands: do not try to drive or walk through flowing water, keep children away from streams and stormwater channels, and stay indoors during lightning.
