Super-subs send Pirates to the top of the Premiership as Durban City finally win under Tinkler

Second-half substitutes Boitumelo Radiopane and Bheki Mabuza scored as Orlando Pirates beat Golden Arrows 2-0 in Soweto to go two points clear of Mamelodi Sundowns.

Sport Desk·Wednesday, 25 November 2026·2 min read

Key points

  • Pirates beat Golden Arrows 2-0 at Orlando Stadium on Tuesday night
  • Both goals came from substitutes, Boitumelo Radiopane and Bheki Mabuza
  • Pirates are top on 28 points, two clear of Mamelodi Sundowns
  • Durban City beat Polokwane City 3-1 for Eric Tinkler’s first league win in charge

Orlando Pirates are top of the Betway Premiership. They beat Golden Arrows 2-0 at Orlando Stadium in Soweto on Tuesday, 24 November, and both goals came from second-half substitutes, Boitumelo Radiopane and Bheki Mabuza, The Citizen reported.

The win lifts the Buccaneers to 28 points, two ahead of Mamelodi Sundowns. Pirates had been second before kick-off, one point behind the champions, according to VAVEL. Arrows stay in the bottom half on 8 points from 10 games.

It caps a strong few days for coach Abdeslam Ouaddou. On Saturday his side beat Stellenbosch 2-0 in the Carling Knockout semi-final to set up a Soweto derby final against Kaizer Chiefs on 12 December.

Patience pays off in Soweto

Ouaddou made five changes from the cup semi-final, and the home side took time to break through. Thapelo Mokobodi went off injured in the 12th minute and was replaced by Thalente Mbatha. Osborn Maluluke missed a clear chance five minutes later, and Sabelo Sithole shot over the bar early in the second half.

Mpho Padime thought he had scored from close range around the hour mark, but it was ruled offside, and Arrows had a penalty appeal turned down. The breakthrough came late, when Radiopane tapped in a low cross from Mbatha. Mabuza made sure of the points in added time.

The Citizen gave no exact minutes for the goals and reported no comments from either coach after the match.

Tinkler gets off the mark

In Durban, Durban City beat Polokwane City 3-1 at Chatsworth Stadium, Eric Tinkler’s first league win since taking charge, AfricaSoccer reported. Siphamandla Ncanana scored in the 28th minute, just after Polokwane keeper Oscarine Masuluke went off injured and was replaced by Lindokuhle Mathebula.

Polokwane’s only goal was an own goal by Brooklyn Poggenpoel, about four minutes before half-time. Gaston Sirino put Durban City back in front just after the hour, his second league goal of the season, and Trevor Mokwena sealed the win late on.

It was only Durban City’s second league win of the campaign. Polokwane have not won since the opening match of the season, and their next fixture is a tough trip to Sundowns.
