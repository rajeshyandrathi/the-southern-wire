Pieter-Steph du Toit flies home, leaving the Boks with a hole at No 7 for the France final

The flanker has withdrawn from Sunday’s Nations Championship final at Twickenham with an injury niggle. Rassie Erasmus has not yet said who will replace him.

Sport Desk·Thursday, 26 November 2026·3 min read

Key points

  • Pieter-Steph du Toit has withdrawn from Sunday’s final against France with an injury niggle and is returning to South Africa
  • Fullback Damian Willemse is also going home after a knock against Ireland
  • Franco Mostert, Cameron Hanekom and bench man Emmanuel Tshituka are among the options at No 7
  • Ireland captain Dan Sheehan has been banned for two weeks for his croc roll on Marco van Staden
  • Kick-off at Twickenham is 18:40 SA time on Sunday, 29 November

The Springboks have lost one of their most important forwards days before the biggest game of their season. Pieter-Steph du Toit, named at blindside flank in Monday’s team for Sunday’s Nations Championship final against France, has withdrawn with an injury niggle and is flying back to South Africa, SA Rugby announced on Wednesday, 25 November.

Fullback Damian Willemse, who was left out of the match-day squad after taking a knock against Ireland in Dublin, is also heading home, The Citizen reported. Planet Rugby said lock Ruan Nortje and prop Ntuthuko Mchunu returned home earlier in the week, while veteran hooker Bongi Mbonambi has joined the group in London.

The squad list now shows “TBC” at No 7. Rassie Erasmus has not decided whether to call up a replacement and will announce his changes later in the week. The Citizen noted that it would be unusual to bring in a player who has not trained with the group so close to a Test.

Who could wear seven

The Citizen named Franco Mostert as the most likely starter. He played lock against Ireland but was left out of the 23 on Monday. Another option is moving Cameron Hanekom, picked at No 8, to the flank, where he played against England earlier this year. Young lock Riley Norton was seen as an unlikely choice.

Then there is Emmanuel Tshituka, who is on the bench and could win his first cap on Sunday. Assistant coach Mzwandile Stick told Planet Rugby that Tshituka’s profile is similar to Du Toit’s and that he brings the same physicality.

Marco van Staden is in doubt. His yellow card in Dublin was upgraded to red, and Planet Rugby said on Wednesday that South Africa were still awaiting the disciplinary verdict. The Citizen listed both Van Staden and Jasper Wiese as unavailable because of bans.

Sheehan banned, fans called to Twickenham

Ireland captain Dan Sheehan, whose yellow card for a croc roll on Van Staden was also upgraded to red, was given a two-week ban at a hearing on Tuesday and has accepted it, SA Rugby Magazine reported.

SA Rugby president Mark Alexander has asked South Africans in Europe to fill the stadium. “The Boks need every possible voice behind them on Sunday,” he told EWN. The final, refereed by England’s Karl Dickson, kicks off at 16:40 UK time, 18:40 in South Africa, on Sunday, 29 November.
