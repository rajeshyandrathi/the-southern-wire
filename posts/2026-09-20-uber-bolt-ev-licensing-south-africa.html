Bolt goes electric in Cape Town as ride-hailing operators press ahead on multiple fronts

Lifestyle Desk·Sunday, 20 September 2026

Bolt has officially launched an electric vehicle category on its platform in South Africa, starting in Cape Town as part of the company's broader strategy to accelerate sustainable urban mobility across the continent. Riders in the Mother City can now request an EV specifically, a first for the local ride-hailing market at this scale.

The rollout is being implemented through a fleet partnership with YugoRide, which will supply and manage the electric vehicles operating on the platform. Bolt's ambitions extend well beyond a pilot: the company plans to scale the category to 500 electric vehicles in South Africa by December 2026, with Johannesburg already earmarked as the next city in line for the expansion.

The timing is notable. Earlier this year, South Africa's ride-hailing operators faced a significant regulatory reckoning, with new national transport rules requiring Uber, Bolt and similar platforms to secure updated operating licences or risk being deemed illegal to operate. That deadline forced both companies to formally engage with the National Public Transport Regulator, submitting applications well ahead of the cutoff — a process that dominated industry headlines for much of the year.

Against that backdrop, Bolt's electric vehicle push reads as more than just an environmental initiative. It's a visible, forward-looking move at a moment when the industry has spent considerable energy simply proving it can operate within the country's evolving legal framework — a way of signalling growth and innovation even while regulatory questions continue playing out in the background.

For riders, the immediate change is straightforward: an EV option now exists in Cape Town, with the promise of quieter, lower-emission trips as the fleet scales up. Whether that convenience extends to Johannesburg on the timeline Bolt has set out will be one of the more interesting local mobility stories to watch heading into 2027.
