Fear grips Ekurhuleni as ninth body found in serial killings probe

Nine women have been found dead across Ekurhuleni, east of Johannesburg, since the first body was discovered in Kempton Park in mid-July. Police have named four of the victims and are holding at least one suspect, while investigators work to establish whether one person or several are responsible. President Cyril Ramaphosa has ordered police to leave "no stone unturned," and a sizeable reward has been offered for information leading to further arrests.
