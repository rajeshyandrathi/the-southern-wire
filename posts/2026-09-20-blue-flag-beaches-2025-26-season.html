South Africa's Blue Flag beaches: 70 stretches of coastline certified for the new season

Lifestyle Desk·Sunday, 20 September 2026

South Africa's 2025/26 Blue Flag and Green Coast season has officially launched, with 97 sites and operators honoured for excellence in coastal management and sustainable tourism. Of those, 70 beaches earned full certification this season — 50 with full Blue Flag status and 20 recognised as pilot sites working toward it.

The programme, managed locally by the Wildlife and Environment Society of South Africa, is no rubber stamp. Blue Flag status requires beaches to meet strict, internationally recognised criteria covering water quality, environmental education, coastal management practices and visitor safety — and crucially, it isn't a permanent award. Every certified beach must reapply and requalify annually, which means this year's list reflects genuinely current standards rather than reputation carried over from previous seasons.

Cape Town features prominently among this year's honourees, with Bikini Beach, Camps Bay, Clifton 4th, Fish Hoek, Llandudno, Melkbosstrand, Muizenberg and Silwerstroomstrand all retaining or earning certification. Further along the coast, Grotto Beach and Kleinmond Beach in the Overstrand Municipality also made this year's list, underscoring that Blue Flag recognition extends well beyond the city's most famous stretches of sand.

The timing lines up neatly with the season ahead. As South Africa moves from spring into the peak summer tourism months, a freshly certified list of Blue Flag beaches gives both local beachgoers and international visitors a reliable shortlist of coastline that meets rigorous environmental and safety benchmarks — genuinely useful information rather than just a feel-good headline.

For coastal municipalities, the recognition also represents a return on sustained investment in water quality monitoring and beach management infrastructure, work that mostly happens out of public view until the annual Blue Flag announcement puts it back in the spotlight.
