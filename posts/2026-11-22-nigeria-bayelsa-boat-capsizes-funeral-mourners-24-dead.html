At least 24 dead after a boat carrying mourners to a funeral capsizes in southern Nigeria

The overcrowded boat overturned on a river in Bayelsa State on Friday. Residents say hundreds may have been on board, and the search for the missing continues.

World Desk·Sunday, 22 November 2026·2 min read

Key points

  • The boat capsized on Friday 21 November between the Oweikorogha and Otuan communities in Bayelsa State
  • Nigeria’s emergency agency said 24 bodies had been recovered by Saturday
  • Police blamed mechanical failure on an overcrowded vessel; 24 people were rescued
  • The driver fled and the boat’s manager has been taken into custody

At least 24 people have died after a boat carrying mourners to a funeral capsized in Bayelsa State in southern Nigeria, Al Jazeera reported.

The boat overturned on Friday 21 November at a river junction between the Oweikorogha and Otuan communities in the Southern Ijaw area. Police said it was travelling from Ayama to Ukubie for a burial ceremony.

Police first reported four bodies, two of them children. By Saturday, the National Emergency Management Agency said 24 bodies had been recovered and that search and rescue work was continuing, so the toll could rise.

How many were on board

Police did not say how many people were on the boat. Al Jazeera reported it was believed to be carrying hundreds of funeral-goers, and some residents told Reuters there were at least 300 people on board. Police said 24 people were rescued: 19 were treated at a local clinic and five were taken to hospital in the state capital.

Police blamed a mechanical failure on an overcrowded vessel. The driver fled the scene, and the boat’s manager has been taken into custody. Police said they were working with local divers and other responders.

A recurring tragedy

Boat accidents are common on Nigeria’s busy rivers and creeks, where many communities depend on water transport. They are usually blamed on overloading, poor maintenance and ignored safety rules. In August, at least 51 people died when a boat sank in Sokoto State, and in January at least 26 drowned when a boat travelling from Jigawa to Yobe State capsized, Al Jazeera reported.

With so many people possibly unaccounted for, the final toll may not be known for days.
