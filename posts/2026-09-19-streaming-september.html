Toy Story 5 and a Supergirl reboot headline September streaming

Culture Desk·Saturday, 19 September 2026

September's streaming calendar is stacked, and two titles stand out for South African audiences with access to global platforms. Pixar's Toy Story 5 arrives on Disney+ later this month, centred on a rescue mission for Jessie that promises to lean into the emotional register the franchise has built its reputation on over three decades.

On the superhero side, a new Supergirl film starring Milly Alcock as the last survivor of Krypton lands on HBO Max. The casting has generated significant buzz, with early reactions suggesting the film leans into a lighter, more character-driven tone than recent entries in the genre — a deliberate contrast that studios are betting will resonate with audiences fatigued by grim reboots.

Beyond the headline releases, the month's slate includes Damien Chazelle's sprawling Hollywood epic making its way to a streaming home, a documentary profile on Netflix, and a relocated romantic comedy sequel that shifts its setting to Italy. Taken together, it is a month that offers something across almost every major genre, timed to catch audiences as the Southern Hemisphere heads into cooler evenings better suited to a night in front of a screen.
