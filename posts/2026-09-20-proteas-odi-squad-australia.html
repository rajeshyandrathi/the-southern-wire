Bavuma names full-strength Proteas squad as Australia arrive for high-stakes ODI series

Sport Desk·Sunday, 20 September 2026

Cricket South Africa has confirmed its one-day international squad for this month's three-match series against Australia, and the headline is stability rather than surprise: Temba Bavuma continues as captain of a group that looks close to full strength for the first time in some time, with both Quinton de Kock and Corbin Baartman recalled.

The timing of the series matters as much as the personnel. These three ODIs — scheduled for 24, 27 and 30 September — arrive as a tune-up ahead of a three-Test series against the same opponent next month, giving the Proteas a rare chance to build momentum and combinations across formats against one of the toughest opponents in world cricket, rather than treating the white-ball leg as a standalone assignment.

De Kock's return in particular sharpens a batting order that has occasionally looked light on proven big-match composure at the top of the innings. Pairing his experience with Bavuma's captaincy gives South Africa a leadership core that has weathered plenty together, at a moment when consistency near the top of the order has been one of the squad's few lingering question marks.

Australia will not arrive underprepared. Few touring sides ever do, and a full-strength Proteas squad taking on a full-strength Australian one produces exactly the kind of series South African cricket fans have been asking for — a genuine measuring stick, rather than a dead rubber, going into a home summer that already has a Test series against the same opposition circled on the calendar.
