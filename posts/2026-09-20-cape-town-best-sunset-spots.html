Where to catch the best sunset in Cape Town this spring

Lifestyle Desk·Sunday, 20 September 2026

As spring evenings stretch later into the day, sunset-watching reclaims its place as one of Cape Town's most reliable rituals — and the city offers an unusually wide range of ways to do it, from a mountainside hike to a rooftop cocktail bar.

For those willing to climb, Lion's Head remains the classic choice, its summit delivering panoramic views across the city bowl, Table Mountain, Camps Bay and the Twelve Apostles range — popular enough for sunset picnics that timing your ascent to avoid the busiest window has become something of a local art form. Kloof Corner offers a shorter, similarly rewarding alternative, while Signal Hill provides free, easy access to 360-degree views over both the city and the Atlantic without requiring a serious hike.

Beach-based options offer a different kind of drama. Sunset Beach in Milnerton has built its name on exactly what it promises: a picture-perfect view of Table Mountain silhouetted against a sky that regularly shifts through pink and purple as the sun drops. Bloubergstrand, further along the same stretch of coast, delivers its own celebrated view of the mountain across the bay from a long stretch of white sand, while Maiden's Cove, tucked between Clifton's beaches and Glen Beach, frames the Twelve Apostles and Camps Bay in dramatic late-afternoon light.

For those who prefer their sunset with a scenic drive attached, Chapman's Peak — winding between Noordhoek and Hout Bay along the Atlantic coast — is regularly rated among the most spectacular marine drives anywhere in the world, and timing the route for late afternoon turns an already impressive drive into something considerably more memorable. Back in the city, the Silo rooftop bar at the V&A Waterfront offers a more polished alternative: cinematic views over the harbour and Table Mountain, cocktail in hand.

Whichever option appeals, the practical advice is the same across all of them: arrive earlier than feels necessary. Cape Town's best sunset spots are well known precisely because they work, and the good vantage points fill up fast once word gets around that conditions look promising.
