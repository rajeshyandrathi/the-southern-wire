Robert Pattinson is the new James Bond, and Denis Villeneuve will direct

Amazon MGM Studios has named the Twilight and The Batman star as the seventh actor to play 007, the first big creative call since it took control of the franchise. Steven Knight is writing the script.

Culture Desk·Friday, 20 November 2026

The longest casting debate in film is over. Amazon MGM Studios announced this week that Robert Pattinson will play James Bond, making him the seventh actor in the official series after Sean Connery, George Lazenby, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton, Pierce Brosnan and Daniel Craig.

The reveal came in a short clip of Pattinson stepping out of the dark in a tuxedo with a pistol raised, followed by the familiar line that James Bond will return. Dune director Denis Villeneuve is directing, from a script by Peaky Blinders creator Steven Knight, with Amy Pascal and David Heyman producing.

Villeneuve said there was extraordinary talent in the running, but there can only be one 007. Pattinson called the role the honour of a lifetime.

This is the first major decision Amazon has made since taking creative control of Bond. It bought MGM in 2022, and in 2025 longtime producers Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson stepped back from running the franchise under a joint venture that gave Amazon MGM the final say.

There is no release date yet, and no other cast has been announced. Reports suggest production could start in 2027, which would put the film in cinemas around 2028. For Pattinson, who went from Twilight heartthrob to Batman, it is another franchise role that will define the next stage of his career.
