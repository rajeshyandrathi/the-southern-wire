Toy Story 5 and A24's biggest hit ever headline a loaded month of streaming

Culture Desk·Sunday, 20 September 2026

September's streaming slate is carrying the weight of two genuine box-office phenomena, and both are landing on subscription platforms within weeks of each other. Pixar's Toy Story 5 arrives on Disney+ after a theatrical run that pulled in $1.13 billion and counting worldwide, cementing the franchise's status as one of the most reliable emotional heavyweights animation has ever produced.

Sharing the spotlight is Backrooms, which lands on HBO Max after becoming A24's highest-grossing film of all time, a staggering $394 million worldwide haul for a studio better known for prestige-scale releases than blockbuster numbers. That result alone marks a meaningful shift in what audiences will turn out for from a studio built on smaller, stranger films — proof that A24's brand can now carry a genuine mass-audience horror hit as comfortably as an awards contender.

Beyond the two headline arrivals, the broader September calendar is stacked with variety: Disney+ premiered the latest Star Wars instalment, The Mandalorian & Grogu, earlier in the month, while Paramount+ brought back the Wayans Brothers horror-spoof franchise with Scary Movie 6. On the VOD side, Anne Hathaway's The End of Oak Street and an Anthony Bourdain biopic, Tony, both became available to rent, giving audiences prestige and genre options in the same week.

Taken together, it's a month that captures streaming's current strategy in miniature: pair a guaranteed emotional crowd-pleaser with a genuine box-office outlier, then surround both with enough range — horror, biography, spoof comedy, franchise spectacle — that almost no subscriber has a reason to look elsewhere for something to watch.
