Chiefs stun Sundowns in extra time to set up a Soweto derby in the Carling Knockout final

Pule Mmodi and Elias Mokwana scored in extra time at Loftus as Kaizer Chiefs beat Mamelodi Sundowns 2-1. They will meet Orlando Pirates in the final on 12 December.

Sport Desk·Tuesday, 24 November 2026·2 min read

Key points

  • Kaizer Chiefs beat Mamelodi Sundowns 2-1 after extra time at Loftus Versfeld on Sunday
  • The match was 0-0 after 90 minutes, played in 31°C heat
  • Orlando Pirates beat Stellenbosch 2-0 in Durban on Saturday to reach the final
  • The final is on 12 December; TimesLIVE says Cape Town Stadium, though EWN says the venue is still to be confirmed

Kaizer Chiefs produced one of their best results in years on Sunday 22 November, beating Mamelodi Sundowns 2-1 after extra time in the Carling Knockout semi-final at Loftus Versfeld in Pretoria, TimesLIVE reported.

The match was goalless after 90 minutes, played in 31°C heat. Three minutes into extra time, Pule Mmodi broke the deadlock from a backheel by substitute Elias Mokwana. Mokwana then scored himself on the counterattack in added time at the end of the first period of extra time. Mothobi Mvala headed a late consolation for Sundowns.

A derby final

Chiefs will face their Soweto rivals Orlando Pirates in the final on 12 December. Pirates reached it by beating Stellenbosch 2-0 in Durban on Saturday. TimesLIVE reported the final will be at Cape Town Stadium, while EWN said the venue was still to be confirmed.

It is a first final for Chiefs coach Fernando da Cruz, whom TimesLIVE described as under pressure before the win. Speaking afterwards, he acknowledged that Sundowns had controlled the game, and said his side had to score when they created chances. He added that the team wanted to lift the trophy, EWN reported.

Why it matters

For Chiefs supporters, a cup final against Pirates is about as big as domestic football gets. Beating Sundowns, coached by Miguel Cardoso, also shows that the side can compete with the country’s dominant club, even if Sundowns had more of the ball.

For Sundowns, the defeat ends one route to silverware this season. Attention now turns to a final that should fill whichever stadium hosts it.
