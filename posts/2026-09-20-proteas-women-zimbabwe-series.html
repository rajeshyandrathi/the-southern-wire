Proteas Women open historic first T20I tour of Zimbabwe with statement wins

Sport Desk·Sunday, 20 September 2026

South Africa's women's team has opened a genuinely historic assignment in style, winning their first two matches of the first-ever bilateral T20 International series between the Proteas Women and hosts Zimbabwe. It's a small but meaningful landmark for a fixture pairing that had, remarkably, never previously featured on the women's international calendar in this format.

The performances backed up the occasion. South Africa's selectors named a 15-player squad for the tour, and it didn't take long for that depth to show: victory in the opening match was built on a standout spell from spinner Nthabiseng Nini, who returned figures of 3 for 17 to strangle the Zimbabwean innings, before the Proteas backed it up with a second straight win days later.

Beyond the results, the tour carries symbolic weight for the growth of the women's game in the region. A first-ever bilateral series is the kind of fixture that expands a format's footprint rather than simply filling a calendar slot, and a visiting South African side delivering consecutive wins on the road sends exactly the signal organisers will want as they look to schedule more fixtures of this kind.

For a Proteas Women's setup that has occasionally lived in the shadow of the men's team's headline fixtures, a statement start to a landmark tour is a reminder that the women's program has its own momentum worth watching in its own right.
