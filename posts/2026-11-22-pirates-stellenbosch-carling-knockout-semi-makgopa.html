Substitute Makgopa scores twice as Pirates beat Stellenbosch to reach the Carling Knockout final

The defending champions won 2-0 in Durban on Saturday night, in a home tie Stellenbosch had to move across the country. Pirates now wait for the winner of Chiefs against Sundowns.

Sport Desk·Sunday, 22 November 2026·2 min read

Key points

  • Orlando Pirates beat Stellenbosch 2-0 at Moses Mabhida Stadium on Saturday 21 November
  • Substitute Evidence Makgopa scored both goals midway through the second half
  • Stellenbosch could not secure a stadium in the Western Cape and played their home tie in Durban
  • Pirates, the defending champions, reach their second Knockout final in a row

Orlando Pirates are back in the Carling Knockout final. The defending champions beat Stellenbosch FC 2-0 in the semi-final at Moses Mabhida Stadium in Durban on Saturday 21 November, with both goals coming from substitute Evidence Makgopa, IOL reported.

The first half ended goalless. Makgopa broke through midway through the second half, scoring in the 69th and 75th minutes according to IOL. News24’s SNL24 reported that the second goal came from a defensive error on a back pass. Stellenbosch’s Obus Arigo missed the target with a late chance in the 88th minute.

Mpho Padime was named player of the match. Pirates left Thalente Mbatha and Oswin Appollis out of the matchday squad after their Bafana Bafana duty, IOL reported.

A home game played in Durban

The match was officially a Stellenbosch home fixture, but it was played in Durban because the club could not secure the DHL Stadium in Cape Town and its own Danie Craven Stadium was ruled ineligible, IOL reported.

Stellenbosch coach Gavin Hunt said the move was disappointing for the club but refused to use it as an excuse. He blamed poor defending for the first goal, and said his side had the best chance of the game before Pirates scored. He told EWN he felt it was a game Stellenbosch should have won, but admitted Pirates were better on the night. He also praised youngster Quwan Plaatjies, saying he had a big future in the game.

Who Pirates will face

Pirates will play the winner of Sunday afternoon’s second semi-final between Kaizer Chiefs and Mamelodi Sundowns at Loftus Versfeld. A Chiefs win would set up a Soweto derby final.

For Stellenbosch, attention turns back to the league, while Pirates take another step towards keeping the trophy they won last season.
