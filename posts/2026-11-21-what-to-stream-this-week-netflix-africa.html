What to stream this week: a mother’s search for forgiveness, a Cinderella twist and a Kenyan-made documentary

Netflix’s new arrivals in Africa this week include the drama On Behalf of My Son, the animated comedy Steps and the documentary short One Last Order, co-directed by Kenya’s Sam Soko. Here is what is coming and when.

Culture Desk·Saturday, 21 November 2026·2 min read

Key points

  • On Behalf of My Son, Steps, The Blue Road and Primetime Preacher$ arrived on Netflix on 20 November
  • One Last Order, co-directed by Kenyan filmmaker Sam Soko, arrives on 24 November
  • The Trials of Karen Read docuseries lands on 23 November; The Hunting Wives season two on 26 November
  • The Altruists, Netflix’s drama inspired by a real fraud case, began streaming on 19 November

If the storms keep you indoors this weekend, Netflix has a busy week of new arrivals in Africa, according to the November schedule compiled by Sinema Focus. The most talked-about of Friday’s releases is On Behalf of My Son, a film about a grieving mother who sets out to meet the families of every victim after her son crashes a bus.

Also new on Friday 20 November is Steps, an animated comedy that retells Cinderella from the side of a stepsister, Lilith, who steals the Fairy Godmother’s wand. For crime fans there is The Blue Road, a series following two detectives investigating murders that cross a border, and Primetime Preacher$, a documentary.

The Altruists, the drama series inspired by a real fraud case, arrived on Thursday 19 November, so it is already available to binge this weekend.

A Kenyan story to look out for

The pick for African viewers comes on Tuesday 24 November. One Last Order is a documentary short co-directed by Kenyan filmmaker Sam Soko and Lauren DeFilippo, and produced by LBx Africa. Soko is best known for Softie, his award-winning documentary about Kenyan activist Boniface Mwangi.

Earlier this month Netflix released two African titles that are still drawing audiences: the Nigerian drama The Covenant Part 2, with South African actors Vuyo Dabula and Sibusiso Jili in the cast, and the South African reality docuseries Stars and Vows: Anele & Buzza, following Anele Mdoda and Buzza James. No new South African production is listed for the coming week.

The rest of the week

Monday 23 November brings The Trials of Karen Read, a true-crime docuseries about the American murder case that divided public opinion, along with a new volume of Sesame Street for younger viewers. On Wednesday 25 November comes The Mosquito Bowl, a film about a wartime American football game.

Thursday 26 November is the busiest day of the week, with the Oslo hospital series Always on Call, the science-fiction series Fool Night, the second season of The Hunting Wives and the Dutch true-crime story Toos: The Van der Valk Kidnapping. Friday 27 November brings the Basque drama The Child and the film The Hunchback, Soul of Paris.

Release dates can differ slightly between countries, so check the app if a title is not showing yet.
